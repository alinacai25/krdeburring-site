Deburring vs Chamfering: What's the Difference?

Published 2026-09-09 · Technical

The two terms get used interchangeably, but they describe different operations with different goals. Knowing the difference saves you from buying the wrong machine — or leaving a finishing step undone.

Definitions

Deburring removes the sharp, raised edges and burrs left by cutting, punching or machining. The goal is safety and cleanliness.

Chamfering deliberately cuts a beveled edge at a defined angle — usually for assembly, stress relief, or aesthetic reasons.

Edge rounding slightly radiuses the edge rather than cutting a straight bevel — common on decorative stainless steel parts.

GoalRemove sharp edges/burrsCut a beveled edgeSoften to a small radius
ResultSmooth, safe edgeDefined angled edgeRounded edge (R0.1–0.5)
Typical useAll cut partsAssembly, aestheticsDecorative panels, hardware
ToolingAbrasive beltBrush roller / toolBrush roller

Do You Need Both?

  • Laser/plasma cut structural parts usually just need deburring.
  • Parts that will be welded, painted or handled benefit from deburring plus a light chamfer.
  • Decorative stainless panels (elevators, appliances) need edge rounding for a uniform hairline look.

One Machine, Both Operations

A belt + roller machine (like the BR series) combines both in a single pass:

  1. The belt removes the burr and oxide scale.
  2. The brush roller then chamfers or rounds the edge and creates a uniform surface finish.

This is why "dual-function" machines are the most popular choice for mixed work — they replace separate deburring and chamfering steps.

FAQ

Is chamfering necessary if I already deburr?

Only if your application needs a defined bevel or a polished edge. Deburring alone is enough for most structural parts.

What edge radius can I achieve?

Typical edge rounding machines produce R0.1–0.5 mm, ideal for decorative and functional finishing.

Can a deburring machine round edges on stainless steel?

Yes — brush rollers are specifically suited to stainless, aluminum and copper edge rounding.
