Selection Guide

How to Select the Right Grit

Five steps to choose the correct grit for your workpiece material and finish requirement.

  1. Identify the workpiece material

    Carbon steel & iron → Silicon Carbide (SiC). Stainless steel & copper → Aluminum Oxide (Al₂O₃). Aluminum → flexible cloth backing.

  2. Check the burr condition

    Heavy / sharp burrs → start with a coarser grit. Medium burrs → medium grit. Light / fine burrs → finer grit.

  3. Define the edge requirement

    Fast burr removal → coarser grit. General deburring → medium grit. Fine edge treatment → finer grit. Edge rounding → medium to fine grit.

  4. Define the surface finish

    Coarser grit = higher material removal + stronger burr removal + more pronounced scratch pattern. Finer grit = smoother surface appearance.

  5. Select the final grit

    Processing TaskRecommended Grit
    Heavy DeburringP60–P80
    General DeburringP80–P120
    Fine Deburring / Edge TreatmentP120–P240
    Fine Surface Preparation / FinishingP180–P400
