About KR Machinery

Your trusted partner for metal deburring, finishing and edge rounding solutions.

Professional Metal Deburring Machine Manufacturer

Founded in 2019, KR Machinery is a manufacturer based in Jinan, Shandong, China, specializing in advanced sheet metal fabrication machines — with metal deburring machines as a core product line.

Our metal deburring machines are designed to remove burrs, slag and oxide scale from laser-cut and plasma-cut parts, while delivering uniform edge rounding and a beautiful hairline surface finish. With BR, BB and R series covering belt + roller, double belt and roller-only configurations, we serve a wide range of materials from thin stainless steel and aluminum to thick carbon steel.

We are committed to continuous innovation and improvement. Our team of experienced engineers and technicians work tirelessly to develop new products and refine existing ones, ensuring our machines stay at the forefront of technology and quality.
